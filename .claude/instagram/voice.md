## How I talk

- **Hinglish in Roman script.** Hindi sentence structure with English nouns and
  business words dropped in: "Yeh business aap India mein start kar sakte ho."
  "Premium modular kitchens ka market tezi se grow kar raha hai." Never pure
  English and never pure shuddh Hindi. A full-English script is the exception,
  and it reads stiffer.
- **Spelling is phonetic and casual:** kar skte ho, bhut, nhi/ni, rha h, hein,
  mtlb, k (for ke), lie (for liye), kia/dia. In **on-screen text and captions**
  use the cleaner spelling (sakte, bahut, nahi, raha hai) so it reads fast. In
  the **spoken script**, leave it loose. That's how I say it.
- **Tum and aap both.** "Aap" to the professional I'm pitching, "tum" when I'm
  hyping a business idea or calling the viewer out ("Dekh bhaai, kaha tu time
  waste kar raha hai").
- **Short stacked lines.** One beat per line. Three-word fragments are fine:
  "No chopping. No measuring. No wastage." / "Simple. High engagement. Aur
  genuinely useful."
- **Ellipses as breath.** "Socho…", "Dekho…", "Aur batata hoon niche…". The
  humanizer will turn "…" into "...". Keep the pause either way.
- **One-word CAPS verdicts.** VIRAL, GOLD, Contrast, LEKIN, RAW, REVERSE
  PSYCHOLOGY. Used to land a point, not to shout a whole line.
- **First person and opinionated.** "Mere 2 cents?" "Likha k le lo." "Me
  guarantee karta hoon." "100% its a viral idea."

## Formats I run (and how each is built)

### 1. The "[Profession] 101" / "7 ideas every [X] should steal" (best performer)

Seven numbered points to one profession. Its shape, taken from the doctor,
dermat, gastro, architect, lawyer, salon, makeup, fitness, pediatrician,
jewellery and realtor scripts:

1. **Hook:** "Agar mujhe kisi [X] ka account grow karna ho, mein yeh karunga…"
   or "Hi, main pediatrician hoon, aise videos start mat karo…"
2. **Points as Stop → Instead:** "Before-after mat dikhao. Customer ki journey
   dikhao." Every point names the bad habit first, then the replacement.
3. **Recurring point types** (pick 5-6 per reel):
   - **Tier list:** rank brands/products S to F. "Maybelline, Lakme, Huda Beauty."
     "Jaquar, Kohler, Grohe, Toto." "Probiotics, ACV, Yakult, Isabgol."
   - **Patient/client questions as hooks:** "Doctor, Minoxidil lagane ke baad
     bhi baal kyun jhad rahe hain?"
   - **Record the real thing:** OPD consultations, site visits, the car ride
     back from a hearing. "Camera lagaa do, mic lagaa do."
   - **Myth-breaking / anti-flow opinions:** "Aloe vera kaam nahi karta."
     "Garmi mein ande nahi khaane chahiye. Myth."
   - **Story, not portfolio:** "Bridal kyun nervous thi, kaise comfortable hui."
   - **Context/dress:** "White coat pehno. Shirt mein video mat banao."
   - **Bad / Good / Excellent ladder:** "Bad: Hi welcome back. Good: Hi main
     Dr. X. Excellent: Kandha rotate karne mein chatakne ki awaaz aati hai…"
   - **Celebrity decode:** "Tendulkar ko tennis elbow tha…", "Kiara, Deepika,
     Ambani wedding ki jewellery decode karo."
4. **Mid-reel lead magnet:** "20/25 hooks ki list banayi hai, comment
   GAS / STONE / PRESCRIPTION / CITY, bhej dunga."
5. **Last point is the offer:** "Busy ho? Isi liye brands hamare saath kaam
   karte hain." / "Hum already [X] ke saath ye system chala rahe hain. $1,000/month.
   Comment CALL / link in bio."

### 2. Business idea from abroad (volume format)

Hook (curiosity or a number) → what the product is → how it works, 3-4 short
lines → **the money line** (cost vs price, margin) → where to sell it in India
→ "India mein koi nahi kar raha" → CTA.

- Money lines are concrete: "100ml = 1000 sprays, 20 paise ki spray, ₹10 mein
  becho, 95% margin." "Billboard 6-10 hazaar mein banta hai, 2-3 hazaar per
  booking." "Machine hotels ko 30-40 hazaar mein becho."
- Always lakh/crore/hazaar and ₹, never "a lot".

### 3. AI tool tutorial

"Ye dekho, AI se ab aap [result] bana sakte ho" → step 1 (Gemini / Nano Banana)
→ step 2 (Google Flow) → "kuch hi seconds mein ready" → **comment KEYWORD, prompt
DM mein bhej dunga.**

### 4. Creator craft / proof reels

"Is video ke 32 million views hain. Pata hai kyun?" → name the principle
(Contrast, Open Loop, Rage bait, Visual hook) → 3 examples → "aap batao
comment mein".

## What I never say / avoid

- **Corporate English CTAs.** "We work with founders and brands who want to
  build ideas like this. If you're building something, you can grow with us
  too." This shows up in ten scripts and it's the flattest line I have. Say it
  in my voice: "Aise ideas ko brand banana hai? Hum yahi karte hain. Link bio
  mein."
- **Caption-speak.** "Elite fitness brands don't just showcase physiques, they
  shape perception." "Leverage…", "elevated storytelling", "exceptional".
  Every caption in the doc that sounds like this is the weakest text in it.
- **Greeting openers.** "Hi my name is…" is D tier by my own ranking hook.
  "Welcome back" is F tier.
- **"In today's busy world…"** and "This might be the smartest X you've never
  seen." I used that one five times. It's stock. Use it at most once a month.
- **Two CTAs in one reel.** Several scripts end with "follow karo aur comment
  karo X". Pick one.
- **Unsourced big claims** ("Nike aur Zara India ki innovations ke peeche
  hain", "35% subsidy", "Gadkari ji khud madad karenge"). If a number or claim
  isn't checked, mark it `{{verify}}`. Getting called out in comments costs
  more than the hook gains.
